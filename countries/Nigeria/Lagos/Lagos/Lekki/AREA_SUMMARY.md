## Key Observations

1. **Lekki is a high-value commercial area** with significant concentration of hospitality businesses (hotels, restaurants, lounges) and real estate agencies.

2. **Strong tech startup presence** - Lekki is home to notable startups like Flutterwave, TeamApt, Chowdeck, Schoolable, and others. The LinkedIn founders/CEOs list includes multiple Y Combinator-backed founders.

3. **Email discovery rate is moderate** (33.3%) - Many Lekki businesses rely on phone/WhatsApp as primary contact channels rather than email, which is consistent with Nigerian business communication patterns.

4. **WhatsApp potential is high** - Even though explicit WhatsApp numbers were rarely listed, most phone numbers in Nigerian format are likely WhatsApp-enabled. Follow-up WhatsApp verification is recommended.

5. **School density is notable** - Lekki has a high concentration of premium international and British curriculum schools, making it an attractive target for education-sector marketing.

6. **Pharmacy chain presence** - Multiple pharmacy chains (Alpha, MedPlus, Mopheth, HealthPlus) have locations in Lekki, suggesting strong healthcare retail infrastructure.
