Cantonments is one of Accra's most prestigious and commercially active neighborhoods. The area attracts a wealthy demographic, including expatriates, diplomats, and business executives.

## Key Findings

### Cantonments Business Landscape
1. **Diplomatic Hub**: Cantonments is home to numerous embassies and international organizations, making it a premium location for professional services.
2. **Education Cluster**: Multiple prestigious international schools (GIS, Safari, Lincoln Community School, Galaxy International, Foy International) are concentrated in the area.
3. **Professional Services Hub**: Major accounting/audit firms (EY, Crowe Veritas, Morrisons & Associates, Transcend) and law firms (Kimathi & Partners, Stobe Law) have offices here.
4. **Hospitality Growth**: New hotel development underway (Hilton Accra Cantonments - coming soon) alongside established properties (Pelican Hotel, Midindi Hotel).
5. **Dining & Lifestyle**: Strong restaurant, cafe, bar, and wellness presence catering to the affluent diplomatic and expatriate community.

### Notable Businesses Discovered
- **EY Ghana** — One of the Big Four accounting firms, headquartered at 60 Rangoon Lane, Cantonments
- **Ghana International School** — One of Ghana's most prestigious international schools
- **Lincoln Community School** — American international school
- **The Pelican Hotel** — Well-rated 4-star hotel
- **East Cantonments Pharmacy** — One of the largest pharmacy chains in Accra
- **Allianz Insurance Ghana** — Major international insurance company
- **Hilton Accra Cantonments** — Upcoming luxury hotel development
